To Agnes

Every morn, facing you evoked the same feelings as that day.
Everything evanescing, the concourse muted
by some abstruse power around you.
I was drawn to you,
like man to the stars.
Incited to face you,
as if the flowers themselves were asking me.
Born to cherish you,
as if fate willed it so.

This morning, I faced you once again.
At peace, as usual.
Blissful, with that same smile on your face.
I held you, adjusted your hair behind your ear.
Touched your lip, if ever so carefully,
and cupped your delicate face with my hand.
I thanked fate for this dayspring.

This evening, I can't face you.
I mistook my warmth for your own,
yet I now share the same chill.
I know you still hold that smile.
I can still sense it,
through the cold mud that now parts us,
by the very same cruel fate.
