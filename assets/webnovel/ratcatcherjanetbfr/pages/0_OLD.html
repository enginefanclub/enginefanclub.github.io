The elevator doors ceremoniously open before Jane once again with a hard, metal creak as they come to rest. She's bored of this by now, having seen the same ritual repeat time and time again across the many floors she's explored. There's something different about this time, however. The knowledge of the looming threat that lies just above her instills a feeling of newfound wonder, a genuine interest born from the anticipation of this being it. The end, full of answers and the retribution that she's been seeking to enact all this time. The one thing that fuelled her.

Revolver in hand, red cap in check and the belief of everyone she's met on the lower floors, she steps into the elevator and, almost gracefully, presses on the final floor. The elevator slams shut behind her and whirs into action almost immediately, as if the very tower itself was forcefully guiding her towards the top with no time to back out. The elevator music shortly kicks in, muting the sond of the light above with a cheery, care-free tune, but this only lasts a couple of seconds before it's suddenly cut with a chirp into slow, methodical clapping.
