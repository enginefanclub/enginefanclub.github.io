Chapter 1

Jane

Agh, ow!

Jane shoots straight up out of bed with a jolt, and a soft metal clinking be heard beside her. Eyes still just barely open from being woken up, she frantically reaches for the light switch, knocking over several additional cans and boxes. The room is filled with sounds of indistinct bangs, clangs and more ripped straight from a cartoon.

She finally hits the switch and we get a good view at her room.

this is a test image and not final

It's a complete mess. There's several boxes and cans of SODA thrown about haphazardly everywhere, the floor practically flooded with them. Every inch of space on the shelves and, really, any surface that has space is overflowing with the stuff. Even the walls are covered with various SODA imagery, only having one or two posters that are from her favourite film series "Gatornado". But that's just the way she likes it. After having spent so long living under a roof where every nook and cranny is obsessively deep cleaned until it's on par with a laboratory, this is her freedom.
