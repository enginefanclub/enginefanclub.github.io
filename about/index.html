about

engine fan club is a group of friends banded together to make fun video games. originally having started on roblox, it has since then moved onto godot to make all future games. there is no specific "theming" to all the games or a running genre that they all fall under, it's all miscellaneous.
inspirations are drawn from basically every little corner of the internet with a widespread variety of interests all being mashed together to produce singular amalgams of them all.
